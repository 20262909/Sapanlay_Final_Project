EXPERIMENT 1

Cables and Connectors

Lab Safety Warning: Always discharge static electricity (ESD) and power down the PSU before disconnecting internal power cables or seating expansion connectors.

Objectives

  • Identify standard internal and external computer cables and connectors used in modern workstations.
  • Differentiate data, display, audio, and power connectors by pin layout and bandwidth capabilities.
  • Apply proper cable management and electrostatic discharge (ESD) precautions during hardware assembly.

Background & Key Connector Reference

Reliable communication between a system unit and its peripherals depends on matching physical connector interfaces with their corresponding signal standards:

  • RJ45 (8P8C Modular Connector): Used with twisted-pair Ethernet cables (Cat5e/Cat6) for wired LAN connectivity up to 1000 Mbps (1 Gbps) or 10 Gbps.
  • HDMI (High-Definition Multimedia Interface): 19-pin digital interface transmitting uncompressed high-definition video and multi-channel digital audio over a single cable.
  • VGA (Video Graphics Array): Legacy 15-pin D-subminiature connector transmitting analog RGBHV video signals (video only, no audio).
  • SATA (Serial ATA): 7-pin L-shaped data connector and 15-pin power connector used for 2.5-inch SSDs, 3.5-inch HDDs, and optical drives.
  • USB Type-C: Reversible 24-pin connector supporting high-speed data transfer, DisplayPort Alt Mode, and USB Power Delivery.
